# CCI Consent History support

## Choose the right support channel

We are happy to help if a step is unclear or something is not working as expected. You can describe the situation in your own words.

For a private question, order or licence issue, use [Services and support](https://coolcatideas.com/en/account/services/) in your Cool Cat Ideas account:

1. Sign in, or create an account if you do not have one.
2. Under **Contact the team**, choose **General question**. If your licence includes active technical support, you can instead choose **Technical support for a licence** and select the relevant **Licence**.
3. Enter a subject and describe the issue. A screenshot and the installed product version can help. Please leave out passwords, licence keys and customer data.
4. Click **Send message**. Your conversation opens after submission, and you can return to it under **Your conversations** on the same page.

You can send a general question without an active support licence. For an installation or technical intervention you already purchased, open the item under **Purchased services** and use its linked conversation.

For an unclear documentation step or a broken link, use **Report an issue** on the [online documentation page](https://coolcatideas.com/en/docs/cci-consent-history/1.0.0/support/). It opens the documentation feedback form. To report a security vulnerability, please [email info@coolcatideas.com](mailto:info@coolcatideas.com) rather than posting the details publicly.

Never include passwords, consent records, customer data, access tokens or production database exports in a public post.

## Information to include

Describe what you tried and what happened. The following details help when
available; please include what you know. If you need to repeat an import or
change historical consent data, use a staging copy:

- module, PrestaShop, PHP and `psgdpr` versions;
- affected shop and language in a multistore installation;
- whether the issue concerns a wording version, customer acceptance or X13 import;
- the expected result, actual result and exact reproduction steps;
- relevant PrestaShop log entries with personal data removed.

Do not paste consent wording tied to a real customer into a public issue. Legal advice, custom import mapping and recovery of damaged source data are separate professional services.
