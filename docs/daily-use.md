# Daily use

## Check what a customer accepted

1. Open **Cool Cat Ideas > CCI Consent History**.
2. Enter the customer's name or email address in the filter below **Customer**.
3. Choose **Search**.
4. Sort a column when you need to review the oldest or newest entries.
5. Open **Show wording** in the matching row.

You will see the saved wording, its version, language and acceptance date. Changing the current text does not alter an older snapshot.

## Publish changed wording

1. Open **Official GDPR Compliance**.
2. Change the account creation consent in each language that needs an update.
3. Save the module settings.
4. Open **CCI Consent History** once and check the current version.

The next logged-in visit also detects the change. A customer who accepted only an older version sees a blocking dialog. The dialog offers two choices: accept the new wording or log out.

## Choose the right kind of text

Use the blocking renewal only for information needed to provide the customer account. Do not place optional newsletter, analytics or advertising consent in this field. Optional consent must remain optional and must not block account access.

## Let customers check their history

1. The customer signs in and opens **My account > Consent history**.
2. Each entry shows the date, wording version, language and source.
3. Choose **Read accepted wording** to read the saved text. Earlier text stays unchanged after an update.
4. Choose **Download my personal data** to open **GDPR - Personal data**.
5. Choose the CSV or PDF download. Consent history is included in the file.

Customers see only their own records from the current shop. A long history is split into pages of 20 entries. The export includes all matching entries, including records on later pages.

When no records are saved, the page explains this. It does not turn the initial wording into an acceptance for an existing customer.

After updating files in an existing installation, open **Cool Cat Ideas > CCI Consent History** once. This connects the new account link and data export without resetting the module or removing history.
